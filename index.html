PROFILE

Joy Culanculan

AI Video Specialist & Video Editor

Creative work, built with an editor’s eye.

I work at the intersection of video editing and generative AI. What I enjoy most is taking an idea that only exists as a script, reference, or rough concept and turning it into a finished video that feels intentional from the first frame to the final cut.

Depending on the project, that can mean realistic AI UGC, VSL creative, product advertising, 2D explainers, stylized 3D character animation, anatomy-driven visuals, or experimental AI formats. I approach them as complete productions rather than disconnected generated clips.

01

My approach

I start with the message and reference, then build the production around them. I plan the shots, lock visual continuity, control movement and performance, and refine the edit until every scene feels like it belongs in the same piece.

02

What I care about

I care about the details viewers feel even when they do not consciously notice them: timing, continuity, believable motion, product visibility, visual consistency, sound, and pacing. My standard is simple — the story should get noticed before the AI does.
